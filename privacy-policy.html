AgroVista · FOODIPEX

Privacy, in plain language.

AgroVista is designed to work offline. It does not request Android permissions and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

Effective August 29, 2026

Scope

This policy covers the AgroVista Android app created for the supplied FOODIPEX company identity. It explains the app's current data practices.

Data collection and sharing

AgroVista has no account system, advertising, analytics, telemetry, tracking SDK, cloud database, or network feature. Its manifest requests no permissions, including no Internet permission.

In short: the app does not send app data to FOODIPEX or a third party.

Information kept on your device

Android local preferences can store:

  • saved learning topics;
  • field-checklist selections;
  • a quick field note; and
  • the light or dark appearance setting.

This information remains in the app's private local storage. Remove it with available reset controls, by clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Actions you choose

The app can place supplied company text on the clipboard, ask Android to open an address in a compatible maps app, or open Android's share chooser. These actions happen only after you select them. The operating system and any app you choose operate under their own privacy practices.

Children and sensitive information

AgroVista is a general business-information and educational-reference app. It does not ask users to submit personal or sensitive information.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated if the app's features or data practices change. A verified developer support email and the public URL of this page must be entered in Google Play Console before publication.
